How to Play

Defend your flock from the wolves.

  • W A S D Move around the farm
  • Mouse Aim your weapon
  • Click Fire your weapon
  • R Reload — only when empty
  • E Build mode / place a building
  • Scroll Cycle the building to place
  • Esc Pause — status & upgrades

Don't shoot your own chickens: You'll lose money!
