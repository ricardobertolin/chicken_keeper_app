# Knowledge Scroll hints

The 44 hints the Knowledge Scroll upgrade reads out, exactly as they appear in
game. The list lives in `js/hints.js`; this file is generated from it, so edit
the source rather than editing here.

The scroll is offered only on the bonus (third) pick screen, only from wave 51
on, and never hands out the same hint twice in a session. Once they have all been
read the upgrade stops being offered. The headings below are the groupings in
the source, not anything the game shows: hints are drawn in random order.

2. Folks say there's a second way to hold the trigger. Gets the shells out a good deal quicker.
3. I hear there are some very secret hats out there.
4. With friendly fire on, you can shoot a hat clean off your buddy's head.
5. If I stand perfectly still in a last stand, maybe they won't see me at all.
6. Every time they drag me back into the fight, the hospital bill gets steeper...

11. Those foxes are worth a fortune!

13. What is that strange upgrade with the chicken on it?
14. Maybe the chicken coop isn't quite what you think it is.
